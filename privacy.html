Privacy Policy

My Little Window · Effective September 19, 2026

My Little Window is a cozy gardening game. It is built to be private by design: the app collects no data about you.

Data collection

My Little Window does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

Your garden save

Your game progress is stored on your device. If you use iCloud, a backup of your save syncs through your own private iCloud account so your garden can follow you to a new device. That data belongs to you, stays inside your Apple account, and is never visible to us.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (gem packs) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Purchases can be managed in your App Store account settings.

Children

Because the app collects no data, no information is gathered from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
