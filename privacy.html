Privacy Policy

My Little Window · Effective September 19, 2026

My Little Window is a cozy gardening game. It is built to be private by design: it collects no personal information, and nothing it does collect can identify you.

Data collection

There are no accounts, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The one thing the game sends is anonymous progression statistics, so we can tell whether the garden is paced well: events like "a player finished the tutorial" or "a room was furnished on day 12", tagged with a random install identifier that is not connected to you, your device, or any account. These are processed by TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service that stores only anonymous aggregates.

You can turn this off any time in the game's Settings ("Anonymous Analytics"), and nothing further will be sent.

Your garden save

Your game progress is stored on your device. If you use iCloud, a backup of your save syncs through your own private iCloud account so your garden can follow you to a new device. That data belongs to you, stays inside your Apple account, and is never visible to us.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (gem packs) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Purchases can be managed in your App Store account settings.

Children

Because the app collects no data, no information is gathered from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
